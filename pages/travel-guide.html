Plan Your Visit

Practical information for visiting Ratanakiri: seasons, getting around, what to bring and how to travel respectfully.

When to GoGetting ThereWhere to StayWhat to BringTravel TipsRespect Local Culture

When to Go

The November–April dry season is generally easier for rural travel. Rain brings greener scenery, but some roads and trails may become muddy. Check current local conditions.

Dry season · November to April

Allow time for travel between sites and confirm trail or waterfall access locally.

Rainy season

Expect lush scenery and possible delays on rural roads. Keep plans flexible.

Weather and river conditions change; ask locally before swimming or setting out on a remote route.

Getting There

Banlung is the provincial capital and a practical base for nearby sites. Visitors commonly travel to the province by road; schedules and journey times change, so confirm transport before departure.

Rural roads can be rough, especially after rain. Arrange a local driver or guide for remote places, and check the route and return plan in advance.

Transport options and journey times change. Check current information locally before you travel.

Where to Stay

Banlung has guesthouses and cafés and is a convenient base for day trips to Yeak Laom Lake and nearby waterfalls.

Some communities offer homestays or community-run services. Arrange these through the host or a local guide, and follow the community's invitation and conditions.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes for trails and steps
  • Light, breathable clothes and a hat
  • Modest clothing for village visits
  • Rain jacket, especially in the rainy season
  • Swimwear for the lake and waterfall pools
  • Insect repellent and sun protection
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Cash in small notes for markets and rural areas
  • Phone or camera, with a power bank

Travel Tips

Carry cash

Small notes can be useful at markets and in rural areas.

Allow extra time

Leave room in your schedule for changing road and weather conditions.

Use local guides for remote places

A local guide can help you plan routes and understand current access.

A relaxed first day

Morning

Walk at Yeak Laom Lake; check local swimming rules.

Late morning

Choose one waterfall and confirm access before travelling.

Afternoon

Visit a community only by invitation or through an arranged host.

Evening

Return to Banlung and plan the next day's route.

Respect Local Culture

Ratanakiri's communities are living places with their own priorities. Let local guidance lead your visit and help protect the natural environment.

Ask before photographing

Ask permission before photographing people, homes or community activities.

Respect ceremonies

Do not enter or record a ceremony unless the community invites you.

Follow access guidance

Stay out of restricted cultural or sacred areas unless permitted.

Leave cultural objects where they are

Do not remove objects, plants or materials from cultural and natural places.

Listen to local hosts

Ask about customs and follow the guidance of community members or your local guide.

Keep nature clean

Stay on permitted paths, take rubbish with you and do not disturb wildlife.
